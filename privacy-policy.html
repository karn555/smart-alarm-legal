5. Permissions Explained

Below is every permission declared in our app and the exact reason we need it. None of these permissions are used to collect personal data from any user, including children.

Permission Why we need it
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show upcoming alarm reminders and snooze notifications on Android 13+ devices. You can revoke this in system settings at any time. This is the only permission we actively request from the user.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM Fire alarms at the exact time you set. Without this, Android may delay or batch the alarm.
VIBRATE Vibrate the device when an alarm rings.
WAKE_LOCK Keep the CPU awake long enough to start the alarm service and ring the alarm reliably.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-schedule your alarms automatically after the device restarts, so you never miss a wake-up.
CAMERA Used only for the optional Photo Challenge — you must take a photo to dismiss the alarm. No images are stored, uploaded, or transmitted. Images are immediately discarded after the challenge is completed.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Run the alarm sound as a foreground service so Android does not silence it in the background.
