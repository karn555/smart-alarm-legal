2. Information We Collect

2a. Data stored locally on your device (never sent to any server)

  • Alarm configurations – times, labels, ringtone URI, repeat days, snooze duration, challenge type & difficulty. Stored in a local Room database.
  • App preferences – settings such as vibration, dark mode, snooze visibility. Stored in SharedPreferences on-device only.
  • Purchase status – whether you have purchased the ad-free upgrade. Stored locally and verified through Google Play Billing.
  • Step count & fitness data – Daily step count, estimated calories burned, active minutes, and your 7-day step history. Stored in a local Room database. Never transmitted to any server.
  • Water intake logs – Daily water consumption entries and your personalised hydration goal. Stored locally on-device only.

This data never leaves your device (except for purchase verification with Google Play). It is not transmitted to any server owned by us.

5. Permissions Explained

Below is every permission declared in our app and the exact reason we need it. None of these permissions are used to collect personal data from any user, including children.

Permission Why we need it
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show upcoming alarm reminders and snooze notifications on Android 13+ devices. You can revoke this in system settings at any time. This is the only permission we actively request from the user.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM Fire alarms at the exact time you set. Without this, Android may delay or batch the alarm.
VIBRATE Vibrate the device when an alarm rings.
WAKE_LOCK Keep the CPU awake long enough to start the alarm service and ring the alarm reliably.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-schedule your alarms automatically after the device restarts, so you never miss a wake-up.
CAMERA Used only for the optional Photo Challenge — you must take a photo to dismiss the alarm. No images are stored, uploaded, or transmitted. Images are immediately discarded after the challenge is completed.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Run the alarm sound as a foreground service so Android does not silence it in the background.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Display the alarm ringing screen as a full-screen notification so you can see and dismiss it immediately, even when the device is locked.
INTERNET Required for displaying advertisements (Google AdMob) and processing in-app purchases (Google Play Billing). We do not transmit any personal data to our own servers.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Check network connectivity before loading ads to avoid unnecessary errors and improve user experience.
com.android.vending.BILLING Process the one-time in-app purchase to remove ads permanently through Google Play Billing.
ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION Required on Android 10+ to access the device's step counter and step detector sensors. Used exclusively by the built-in Step Counter feature, which the user must manually start. Without this permission, step tracking is fully disabled and the rest of the app continues to work normally. Not used for background activity monitoring or any form of profiling.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH Allows the step counting service to run as a foreground service of type "health" while the user has an active walking session. This keeps the step counter running reliably and shows a persistent notification so the user is always aware tracking is active. The service stops immediately when the user taps Stop or closes the session.
RECORD_AUDIO Used only for the Voice Recall alarm challenge — the user must speak back a memorised sentence to dismiss the alarm. Audio is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. Microphone access ends as soon as the challenge is completed or the alarm is dismissed.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
(Android 9 and below only)		 Required on older Android versions (API 28 and below) to save the reference photo used in the Photo Challenge. Not requested on Android 10 or later. The photo is stored locally and only used to verify the user's location when dismissing the alarm.
